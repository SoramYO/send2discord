Privacy Policy for Send2Discord

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

Thank you for using Send2Discord ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclosure, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension.

1. Information We Collect

Send2Discord is designed to respect your privacy. We collect the minimum amount of information necessary to perform the extension's function.

  • User-Submitted Content: We process the text, URLs, or media you explicitly select and choose to send to Discord via the extension.
  • Technical Data: We do not collect analytics, IP addresses, or tracking data about your browsing habits.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you select is transferred solely for the purpose of sending it to the Discord Webhook URL you have configured. We do not store your data on our servers. The data is transmitted directly from your browser to Discord's servers.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension interacts with Discord. By using this extension, you are also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy regarding the data received by their platform.

Please review Discord's Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle your data.

4. Data Storage

Send2Discord stores your Discord Webhook URL locally in your browser's synchronized storage (Chrome Sync or Edge Sync) for your convenience. This ensures your settings are preserved across your devices. We do not have access to this data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
